## Question

What should case study visual showcases look like? Specifically:

1. How do we present mockups? (device frames? full-width? staggered?)
2. How do we show before/after comparisons? (slider? side-by-side? overlay?)
3. How do we integrate wireframes and user flows? (inline images? expandable sections?)
4. How do we make visual content feel premium, not just "here are some screenshots"?
5. What's the right balance between text and visuals?

Build a rough prototype for one case study section showing these patterns.
