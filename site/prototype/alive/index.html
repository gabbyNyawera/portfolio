One hand, one voice, one quiet machine. The script signs, the serif speaks, the sans does the admin — three jobs, no overlap.

Lobster Two · Bodoni Moda · Jost

01 — The hand

Lobster Two

The hero, the wordmark, the sign-off, the one word you want remembered. Bold, wavy, unmistakably drawn. It never sets body copy and never appears twice in the same view.

02 — The voice

Bodoni Moda

Project titles, pull quotes, subheads, the lede. A luxe didone with real hairline contrast and an italic worth using. This is what keeps the page looking edited rather than decorated.

03 — The machine

Jost

Navigation, labels, body copy, buttons, metadata. Geometric and quiet, so the other two can be loud. It is the only one of the three that has to work at 12px.

The sign-off

And when the page ends, the hand comes back:

let’s make something.

Twice per page, no more: once at the top as the voice, once here as the signature. The restraint is what keeps a script from turning into costume jewellery.

The pairing

A hand, a voice, and a machine that knows its place.

SlotFaceWhy it earns the job
Hand Lobster Two 700 Bold and wavy like the references, and it survives being set at 8rem. One weight, so it can only ever be the loud thing.
Voice Bodoni Moda High-contrast didone with an optical-size axis and a real italic — the editor in the room.
Machine Jost Geometric sans that stays out of the way at 12px and never competes with a swash.

Rules

  • Script twice per page. Hero and sign-off.
  • Serif never below 1.25rem.
  • Sans never sets a headline.
  • One swash per screen, no exceptions.

Weight

Lobster Two 47kb, Bodoni Moda 100kb, Jost 26kb. About 173kb for the whole cast — heavier than Instrument Serif alone, and the price of having three distinct voices.

Licence

All three are OFL and already self-hosted here. If you later buy a retail script, it drops into the hand slot and the other two do not move.
