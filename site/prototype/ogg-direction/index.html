The direction

Three voices,
not three
decorations.

The pairing you picked is a hierarchy: a theatrical display face, a book face that carries real reading, and a neutral geometric sans for the machinery around it. Your current stack collapses the first two into Instrument Serif, which is why the reference feels more resolved than the site does.

Two of the three originals are commercial — Ogg is Sharp Type, Avenir is Monotype — so they cannot be self-hosted here. Everything on this page is SIL Open Font Licence, set at the same sizes on the same copy, so the comparison is honest.

The display slot is the interesting decision. The other two slots have a clear winner each, and they are already on the site.

01 — Display

Ogg

The voice. Headlines, project titles, the italic word inside a heading, the monogram. Never body copy — the hairlines disappear below about 1.75rem.

The book. Ledger text, case-study paragraphs, pull quotes, captions that need to be read rather than admired. This is the role your site currently has no face for.

Slot 01 — the real decision

Which face plays Ogg?

Same word, same size, same italic line. Ogg is a didone with calligraphic terminals — these four are the honest open-licence neighbours, ordered from literal to warm.

Bodoni Moda OFL · 100kb

Ogg

feel alive

The literal swap. A true didone with an optical-size axis and a sharp nineteenth-century italic — closest to Ogg's genre. Costs: fragile under 2rem, and it reads fashion editorial before it reads product design.

Playfair Display OFL · 76kb

Ogg

feel alive

The safe one. Variable 400–900, a sturdy italic, and it holds a three-line headline at 2.5rem where Bodoni Moda thins out. Costs: it is the default editorial serif on the internet, and its contrast is gentler than Ogg's.

Fraunces OFL · already hosted

Ogg

feel alive

The calligraphic one. At display optical sizes its terminals flare like pen strokes, and the italic genuinely swashes — the closest thing to Ogg's personality that is free. Costs: warmer and slightly quirkier than the reference.

Instrument Serif OFL · shipping now

Ogg

feel alive

The incumbent. One weight, one italic, high contrast, and it already makes the Ogg-ish gesture. Costs: no weight range, no optical sizing, so every headline has the same temperature.

Slot 03 — the machinery

Which sans keeps it friendly?

Avenir's trick is geometry you can read: circular bowls, humanist proportions, open apertures. Judge these at 11px uppercase, where they actually live.

If you adopt it

Fraunces, Newsreader, Figtree — and four rules

Trio C keeps the reference's structure without borrowing its mood. Fraunces carries the drama, Newsreader does the reading, Figtree stays out of the way. All three are already licensed for this site, and two are already in the font folder — the only new file is Figtree at 20kb, against the ~30kb of Instrument Serif latin files it replaces.

SlotFaceWhy this one
Display Fraunces Most contrast in this line-up, with flared terminals and a genuinely swashy italic. Behaves at 1.75rem, where Bodoni Moda does not.
Serif Newsreader Real italics and a full weight range, so emphasis has somewhere to go — which Baskervville's 400-only files do not allow.
Sans Figtree Closest to Avenir's geometry-plus-warmth. Jost is more convincing as a label but colder across the whole UI.

1 — Display ceiling

Fraunces never sets below 1.75rem. Under that, the softness reads as a mistake rather than a flourish.

2 — Serif owns prose

Paragraphs, ledes and quotes are Newsreader. If Inter keeps the lede, you have bought a book face and used it as a label.

3 — Sans stays small

Figtree handles nav, eyebrows, buttons and metadata. It never sets a headline — that is how the three roles stay legible.

4 — One italic per screen

The italic is the loudest thing on the page. Once per view — inside the h1, or on the sign-off. Not both.

What it costs

Adopting trio C in source.css means three token lines — --font-display, --font-serif, --font-sans — plus moving .lede and the case-study prose onto the serif. Instrument Serif and its four subset files leave the page weight entirely.

What is still commercial

Nothing here needs a licence beyond the OFL. If you later buy Ogg, it drops into the display slot and the other two roles do not move — which is the useful thing about deciding structure before you decide faces.
