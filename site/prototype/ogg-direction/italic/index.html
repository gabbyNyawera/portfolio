The target

The swash is
the whole point.

Ogg is not really a didone with a nice italic bolted on. Its italic is a chancery hand — pen-written structure, sweeping entry strokes, wide bowls — drawn at didone contrast. That combination is rare, and almost nothing free attempts it.

So the honest framing is not “which of these is Ogg”. It is which trait you are actually buying: the gesture of the swash, the contrast of the hairline, or a family with enough weights to run a whole site.

No free face delivers all three. Each row below is scored against the reference on that basis, and the ranking is mine, not a spec sheet.

The call

Free gets you about seventy per cent

If the site stays open-licence, switch the display slot to Playfair Display and keep Newsreader and Figtree underneath. You gain the swash, the weight range and the italic emphasis; you lose Ogg's particular softness, which no free face has.

If you value…PickBecause
The gesture Playfair Display Closest swash plus a usable 400–900 range. The default answer, and the right one.
The contrast Bodoni Moda Sharpest hairlines and an opsz axis, at the cost of any calligraphic warmth.
Shopping nothing Instrument Serif Already hosted and already close. You would be buying range, not shape.

If you want the real thing

Ogg is a Sharp Type licence. Web use is typically sold per traffic tier and does not permit redistributing the files, so self-hosting stays inside your account with the foundry. Ask them directly for a quote rather than guessing from a price list — and ask what happens at the next tier, because a portfolio that gets shared can cross one.

Adjacent faces to look at in the same room: Canela (Commercial Type), Editorial New and Migra (Pangram Pangram), Reckless (Displaay), Domaine Display (Klim). All are didone- or chancery-adjacent display families, not substitutes — look at their italics specifically.

The hybrid that actually works

Buy one licence for the display slot only. Keep Newsreader and Figtree, which are free and already carrying the reading. That is roughly 30kb of free text faces plus a single licensed display file — the smallest possible commercial footprint, and the one place where a bought face earns its money.

Set a display face at 1.75rem and up, italic once per screen, and everything below that in Newsreader. Do not let a bought display face set body copy; that is how licences get expensive and pages get worse.
