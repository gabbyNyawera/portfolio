Display · the warm option

Thin, and impossible
to ignore.

The six you picked are not one direction. Four are hairline serifs and two are brush scripts — and they do different jobs on a page. That split is the useful thing here.

The thin serifs are a display face: headlines, project titles, the monogram. The scripts are a signature: your name, a sign-off, a wordmark — never a paragraph.

Both racks translate to free faces far more faithfully than Ogg did. Delicate type is a tradition, not a trick, so there are good open revivals.

Rack A — display

The hairline serif

After Five, Classico, Shefian, Editorial. High contrast, very thin stems, often an italic that reads as handwriting. Belongs at 2rem and up, never in body copy.

Rack B — signature

The brush script

Vintage Goods, Maisonette, Breathing, Made by Nature. Warm, fast, hand-made. At most twice a page, and never anywhere a reader has to work.

Where it breaks

The hairline has a floor

Same face, same weight, four sizes. This is the argument for a separate text serif — the display face does not degrade gracefully.

5rem Quietly distinctive As intended. Editorial, unhurried.
2.5rem Quietly distinctive Still holding. Good for project titles.
1.5rem Quietly distinctive Hairlines start to shimmer on Windows.
1rem Quietly distinctive Stop. This is a reading size, not a display size.

The call

Cormorant Light italic, Italiana for caps, Norican for the signature

That combination covers four of your six references with two OFL families and one 10kb caps face — and it keeps the site's licence stack clean, which the Fontshare route does not.

The honest economics

Unlike Ogg, these are cheap. Classico is $18 and Shefian $19 on the marketplaces you screenshotted. At that price a free approximation is a false economy if you love the exact drawing — buy it.

Check one thing first: retail display licences often cover desktop use and not @font-face embedding. You may need an extended or web licence, which is a different price. Ask before you build on it.

If you stay open-licence

--font-display becomes Cormorant at 300 italic; keep Newsreader for anything below 1.75rem, and add Norican as a one-off signature — hero once, footer once, nowhere else.

Italiana is worth having regardless: 10kb for a wordmark and spread titles is the cheapest upgrade on this entire page.
