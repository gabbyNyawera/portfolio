Button

One press · three rules

Primary press

One press per section. An ink pill on paper: it lifts and deepens on hover. Inside a dark section it turns to glass so it still floats above the ground.

Rule & type

Tertiary actions stay typographic — before we begin.

On dusk

On dusk the glass fills with a lighter tint and a brighter border. The rules stay rules.

Don'ts

  • Roman G inside the plate — the italic is the mark.
  • No outline, rotation, stretch or new radius.
  • Never change or remove the stop.
  • Never on a photograph, tint band or colour fill.
  • No second symbol. It is the only mark on the page.

I'm Gabrielle, a product designer working across UX, UI, and design systems. I care about the details that make complex things feel obvious.

My approach is warm and editorial: understand the problem, reduce it to what matters, then design the structure before the surface. I like work that feels useful, human, and quietly distinctive.
