Rules

Gold never sets text on cream

At 1.96:1 it fails. Use #8A5A00 — the same hue, darkened to 5.78:1 — and keep true gold for fills.

Black and oxblood never meet

They sit 1.47:1 apart. Black owns type on cream; oxblood owns whole sections. No oxblood captions under black headlines.

One gold moment per page

A single full-bleed block, or the one primary button. Two gold blocks and the colour stops being a signal.
