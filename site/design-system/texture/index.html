The frame

Depth is light
and structure.

Pattern alone flattens. A surface reads as deep when something lights it, something rules it, and one thing is allowed to be loud.

    Paper grain on body, one overlay, very low opacity. This is the floor — everything else sits on it.

  2. 02

  4. 04

Verdict

What I would ship

Four treatments are ready to go into the system today. Four need a page to prove themselves on. Four break the rules as written.

Adopt

  • Halftone screen

    Gives every placeholder artwork a print identity. Replaces the flat gradient box.

  • Letterpress cards

    One inset highlight and one inset shadow. Depth for free, no new colour.

  • Ledger rules

    Puts Education and Interests on ruled paper. Structure, not ornament.

  • Section seam

    A 6rem blend instead of a hard band edge. Removes the last hard line on the page.

Test next

  • Dusk horizon

    Makes the footer read as a place rather than a dark block.

  • Arch bloom

    Deepens the hero's existing glow into layered arches.

  • Rail edge fade

    Cards dissolve at the viewport edge instead of being sliced.

  • Registration marks

    Crop marks on media frames. Small signal that the placeholder is deliberate.

No

  • Animated gradient mesh

    Loops are banned for everything but the scroll cue.

  • Gold gradient type

    Contrast collapses below 3:1 mid-gradient. Fails the type rules.

  • Full-page vignette

    Deadens the cream and makes the paper look dirty.

  • Marbled endpaper

    Fights the grain overlay. Two textures, one surface.

Next step

Pick two from the adopt list, and I'll wire them into the real components — tokens and utilities first, then the hero, artwork and band seams.

Each one becomes a named utility in globals.css, so the system stays inspectable and nothing gets hand-painted into a page.
