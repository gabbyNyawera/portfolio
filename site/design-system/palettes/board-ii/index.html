Palette options · board II

Six ways to hold the heat.

Same structure as the live system — paper, ink, one pop, and a dusk inversion. The board supplies six different families to put inside it. Contrast figures are sampled against each palette's own paper and dusk.

The real decision

All six keep the system's rule: one pop per page, a dusk variant that is the same material after dark, and a secondary colour that stays inside artwork. The board is louder than any single accent — the temptation is to use two at once. That is a rule change, not a palette swap.
