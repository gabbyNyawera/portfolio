The rule that makes this work

Colour is a block.
Type stays black.

On white paper, a saturated accent cannot be body copy — hot pink is 3.5:1, teal 2.9:1, acid yellow 1.3:1. So the accent arrives as a filled field or a pill, and the reading type is black on white. Links use a darkened sibling of the accent (the third figure under each poster) so they stay legible without dulling the block. Vermilion at 5.0:1 is the one that can also carry small text.

That is the whole difference from the last attempt: not less colour, but colour in a bigger area with less of it turned into small text.
